GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

What does the self-watering kit do?

It is designed to help provide water gradually to the soil and reduce frequent manual watering. Follow the product instructions for setup and use.

Why include a soil-moisture indicator?

The indicator helps you check soil moisture and make a more informed watering decision.

Is GrowEase suitable for beginners?

It is designed as a simple plant-care aid. Different plants have different watering needs, so check your plant's requirements too.

Does it replace all plant care?

No. Plants still need suitable light, nutrients, drainage and occasional checks. GrowEase is intended to support watering, not replace all plant care.
